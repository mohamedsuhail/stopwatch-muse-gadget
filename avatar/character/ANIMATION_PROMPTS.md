# Animation prompts

Complete prompts for new clips of the MOBOT astronaut, one per video, for an
image-to-video model (Veo, Kling, Runway, Luma). Each prompt stands on its own: copy
it as it is.

## How to generate every clip

1. **Make the anchor frame once** (the first prompt below) and save it as
   `avatar/character/anchor.png`. Every clip starts and ends on this exact image.
2. **Give the model `anchor.png` as both the first and the last frame** (Veo's and
   Kling's "first and last frame" option). This is what makes the clips loop and join:
   the motion leaves the pose and comes back to it exactly. (`goodnight` takes only a
   first frame; it ends asleep.)
3. **Settings:** 1280×720 (16:9; `make_anims.py` crops the centre square) or 1:1; the
   length and frame rate given with each clip; camera locked; no audio.
4. **Keep a clip only if** its first and last frames match the anchor (same size, same
   spot, feet in the same place), no prop is left in his hands at the end, nothing
   touches the frame edges, and the background stays pure black. Save it as
   `avatar/character/clips/<file>.mp4`, then run
   `uv run --with pillow python avatar/character/make_anims.py` (it also matches each
   clip's size to idle).

The animation partition holds about 7.6 MB, and today's 8 clips fill it at 24 fps.
The calm loops are marked 15 fps below; a 15 fps clip uses about 60% of the space. To
fit many idle variations the partition can grow (the flash is 16 MB): ask first.

## Anchor frame (image)

**File:** `anchor.png`, 1280×720, a still image.

**Prompt:**

> A single still image of a cute 3D toy astronaut character with a soft matte vinyl-and-clay look, like a premium collectible figure. He has a round white helmet with an orange rim around a clear glass visor; two short teal antennae on top of the helmet, each ending in a glossy teal ball; orange-and-yellow round ear pieces on the sides of the helmet. Inside the visor, a cream, round face framed by fuzzy beige lining, two small black dot eyes, soft rosy cheeks and a tiny curved smile. He wears a puffy orange spacesuit with teal-and-white stripes at the wrists, knees and ankles, fuzzy beige cuffs at the wrists, orange gloves and orange boots with white soles; a white chest control panel with a round rocket badge and small orange, teal and yellow buttons, connected by a curved yellow hose; a white-and-teal backpack with yellow dials and an orange vent on its side. Chubby, friendly proportions: a big head and a short, rounded body. Full body in frame, centred horizontally, facing the camera, his feet near the bottom of the frame with a small margin of black below them and the antennae well inside the top. He stands on an invisible floor in front of a pure solid black background (#000000): no floor, ground plane, shadow, horizon, stars or any other object. Soft studio key light from the front left with a gentle rim light from behind; the lighting never changes. The camera is locked off: no zoom, pan, tilt, dolly, cut or shake, and he stays the same size and in the same place in the frame. He stands in the neutral pose: upright, arms relaxed at his sides with hands slightly forward, looking straight at the camera with a small closed-mouth smile. Exactly the character in the reference image, with the same proportions and colours.

**Negative prompt:** text, letters, logos, watermark, subtitles, extra characters, a second astronaut, background objects, floor, ground, shadow on the floor, stars, space backdrop, planets in the background, cropped feet, cropped antennae, extra limbs, extra fingers, helmet removed, visor opening, realistic human face, prop left in hand at the end, lighting change
